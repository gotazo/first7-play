---
title: "Revelation Journey"

description: "Journey through the visions, messages, and closing promises recorded in the Revelation of Jesus Christ."

difficulty: "medium"

book: "Revelation"

questions:

  - question: "Who was the Revelation of Jesus Christ given to?"

    choices:
      - John
      - Peter
      - Paul
      - James

    answer: "John"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 1:1"

    explanation: "The Revelation of Jesus Christ was sent and signified by His angel unto His servant John."

  - question: "On which island was John when he received the Revelation?"

    choices:
      - Patmos
      - Crete
      - Cyprus
      - Malta

    answer: "Patmos"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 1:9"

    explanation: "John said that he was in the isle that is called Patmos for the word of God, and for the testimony of Jesus Christ."

  - question: "How many churches in Asia were addressed in the opening of Revelation?"

    choices:
      - Seven
      - Five
      - Ten
      - Twelve

    answer: "Seven"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 1:4"

    explanation: "John addressed the seven churches which are in Asia."

  - question: "Which church was told that it had left its first love?"

    choices:
      - Ephesus
      - Smyrna
      - Philadelphia
      - Laodicea

    answer: "Ephesus"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 2:4"

    explanation: "The Lord said to the church of Ephesus, Nevertheless I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love."

  - question: "What did John see in the right hand of Him that sat upon the throne?"

    choices:
      - A book sealed with seven seals
      - A golden crown
      - A sword
      - A golden lampstand

    answer: "A book sealed with seven seals"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 5:1"

    explanation: "John saw a book written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals."

  - question: "Who was found worthy to open the book and loose the seven seals?"

    choices:
      - The Lamb
      - One of the elders
      - An angel
      - John

    answer: "The Lamb"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 5:5-7"

    explanation: "The Lion of the tribe of Judah had prevailed to open the book, and John saw the Lamb take the book from the right hand of Him that sat upon the throne."

  - question: "What was the name of the holy city that John saw coming down from God out of heaven?"

    choices:
      - New Jerusalem
      - New Bethlehem
      - Holy Zion
      - Heavenly Jerusalem

    answer: "New Jerusalem"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 21:2"

    explanation: "John saw the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband."

  - question: "How many gates did the holy city have?"

    choices:
      - Twelve
      - Seven
      - Ten
      - Twenty-four

    answer: "Twelve"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 21:12"

    explanation: "The city had a great and high wall with twelve gates, and twelve angels at the gates."

  - question: "What was on either side of the river of the water of life?"

    choices:
      - The tree of life
      - The golden altar
      - Seven candlesticks
      - The throne of the Lamb

    answer: "The tree of life"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 22:2"

    explanation: "In the midst of the street and on either side of the river was the tree of life, which bore twelve manner of fruits."

  - question: "What did Jesus say at the end of the Revelation?"

    choices:
      - Surely I come quickly
      - Behold, I make all things new
      - Fear not
      - It is finished

    answer: "Surely I come quickly"

    scripture:
      reference: "Revelation 22:20"

    explanation: "He which testifieth these things said, Surely I come quickly. The book closes with the response, Even so, come, Lord Jesus."
---
