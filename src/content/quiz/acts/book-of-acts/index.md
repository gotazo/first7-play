---
title: "Book of Acts"

description: "Follow the growth of the early Church from Pentecost to Rome."

difficulty: "easy"

book: "Book of Acts"

questions:

  - question: "What event marked the beginning of the Church's public witness?"

    choices:
      - Pentecost
      - Passover
      - Ascension
      - Tabernacles

    answer: "Pentecost"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 2:1-4"

    explanation: "The Holy Spirit was poured out upon the believers at Pentecost."

  - question: "Who preached the powerful sermon on the Day of Pentecost?"

    choices:
      - Peter
      - John
      - Philip
      - Paul

    answer: "Peter"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 2:14"

    explanation: "Peter stood up and boldly preached Christ to the crowd."

  - question: "Who became the first Christian martyr?"

    choices:
      - Stephen
      - Philip
      - Barnabas
      - James

    answer: "Stephen"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 7:59-60"

    explanation: "Stephen was stoned after faithfully testifying about Christ."

  - question: "Which evangelist explained Scripture to the Ethiopian eunuch?"

    choices:
      - Philip
      - Peter
      - Paul
      - Silas

    answer: "Philip"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 8:35"

    explanation: "Philip preached Jesus beginning from the passage in Isaiah."

  - question: "What was Saul doing when Jesus appeared to him on the road to Damascus?"

    choices:
      - Persecuting Christians
      - Preaching
      - Fishing
      - Building synagogues

    answer: "Persecuting Christians"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 9:1-5"

    explanation: "Saul was travelling to arrest believers when Christ appeared to him."

  - question: "What new name became associated with Saul after his conversion?"

    choices:
      - Paul
      - Silas
      - Titus
      - Timothy

    answer: "Paul"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 13:9"

    explanation: "Saul, also called Paul, became the great missionary to the Gentiles."

  - question: "What was the main purpose of Paul's missionary journeys?"

    choices:
      - Preach the Gospel
      - Collect taxes
      - Build palaces
      - Train soldiers

    answer: "Preach the Gospel"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 13:47"

    explanation: "Paul travelled widely proclaiming Christ and planting churches."

  - question: "What important issue was discussed at the Jerusalem Council?"

    choices:
      - Whether Gentiles must keep the Law of Moses
      - Choosing a new king
      - Building a temple
      - Dividing Israel

    answer: "Whether Gentiles must keep the Law of Moses"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 15:1-29"

    explanation: "The council addressed how Gentile believers should be received."

  - question: "What happened to Paul while travelling to Rome?"

    choices:
      - He was shipwrecked
      - He became king
      - He returned to Jerusalem
      - He built a synagogue

    answer: "He was shipwrecked"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 27:41"

    explanation: "Paul survived a shipwreck on the journey to Rome."

  - question: "Where does the Book of Acts conclude?"

    choices:
      - Rome
      - Jerusalem
      - Antioch
      - Ephesus

    answer: "Rome"

    scripture:
      reference: "Acts 28:30-31"

    explanation: "Acts ends with Paul preaching the kingdom of God in Rome."
