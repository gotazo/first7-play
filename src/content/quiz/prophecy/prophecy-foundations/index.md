---
title: "Prophecy Foundations"

description: "Explore the prophets, visions, and prophetic writings found throughout Scripture."

difficulty: "medium"

book: "Prophecy"

questions:

  - question: "According to Scripture, how did holy men of God speak prophecy?"

    choices:
      - As they were moved by the Holy Ghost
      - According to their own wisdom
      - According to the commandment of kings
      - According to the traditions of men

    answer: "As they were moved by the Holy Ghost"

    scripture:
      reference: "2 Peter 1:21"

    explanation: "The KJV says that holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost."

  - question: "Which prophet was told to write the vision and make it plain upon tables?"

    choices:
      - Habakkuk
      - Jeremiah
      - Isaiah
      - Ezekiel

    answer: "Habakkuk"

    scripture:
      reference: "Habakkuk 2:2"

    explanation: "The Lord answered Habakkuk and told him to write the vision and make it plain upon tables."

  - question: "Which prophet saw four great beasts come up from the sea?"

    choices:
      - Daniel
      - Ezekiel
      - Isaiah
      - Zechariah

    answer: "Daniel"

    scripture:
      reference: "Daniel 7:2-3"

    explanation: "Daniel saw in a vision by night four great beasts come up from the sea."

  - question: "Which prophet was shown a valley full of very dry bones?"

    choices:
      - Ezekiel
      - Daniel
      - Jeremiah
      - Isaiah

    answer: "Ezekiel"

    scripture:
      reference: "Ezekiel 37:1-3"

    explanation: "The hand of the Lord carried Ezekiel into a valley which was full of bones."

  - question: "Which prophet recorded the sign of a virgin conceiving and bearing a son?"

    choices:
      - Isaiah
      - Jeremiah
      - Micah
      - Joel

    answer: "Isaiah"

    scripture:
      reference: "Isaiah 7:14"

    explanation: "Isaiah recorded the sign that a virgin would conceive and bear a son, and call his name Immanuel."

  - question: "Which prophet named Bethlehem Ephratah as the place from which a ruler would come forth?"

    choices:
      - Micah
      - Isaiah
      - Jeremiah
      - Zechariah

    answer: "Micah"

    scripture:
      reference: "Micah 5:2"

    explanation: "Micah wrote that out of Bethlehem Ephratah would come forth one that was to be ruler in Israel."

  - question: "Which prophet said that God would pour out His spirit upon all flesh?"

    choices:
      - Joel
      - Amos
      - Hosea
      - Malachi

    answer: "Joel"

    scripture:
      reference: "Joel 2:28"

    explanation: "Joel recorded that God would pour out His spirit upon all flesh, including sons and daughters who would prophesy."

  - question: "What was written on the roll shown to Ezekiel?"

    choices:
      - Lamentations, and mourning, and woe
      - Songs of praise and thanksgiving
      - The names of the twelve tribes
      - The laws of Moses

    answer: "Lamentations, and mourning, and woe"

    scripture:
      reference: "Ezekiel 2:9-10"

    explanation: "The roll was written within and without, and there was written therein lamentations, and mourning, and woe."

  - question: "To which city was Jonah commanded to cry against?"

    choices:
      - Nineveh
      - Babylon
      - Jerusalem
      - Damascus

    answer: "Nineveh"

    scripture:
      reference: "Jonah 1:2"

    explanation: "The LORD commanded Jonah to arise and go to Nineveh and cry against it because of its wickedness."

  - question: "According to Amos, to whom does the Lord reveal His secret?"

    choices:
      - His servants the prophets
      - The kings of Israel
      - The priests of Jerusalem
      - The armies of Israel

    answer: "His servants the prophets"

    scripture:
      reference: "Amos 3:7"

    explanation: "Amos wrote that the Lord God revealeth His secret unto His servants the prophets."
---
